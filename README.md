# .github
Here we present the work of the Research Group on Urban Structure and Policy (USP) at Leibniz-Institute of Ecological Urban and Regional Development, Dresden, Germany.

The research group "Urban Structure and Policy"[https://www.ioer.de/en/research/spatial-information-and-modelling/urban-structure-and-policy] aims at fostering the integration of approaches of geoinformatics and planning science at IOER’s Research Area Spatial Information and Modelling (FB M). We contribute to the creation of empirical and conceptual knowledge for a transition towards sustainable urban form. The group develops approaches to integrate and contextualise spatial information with the aim to address causal relations between urban structure and policy.
